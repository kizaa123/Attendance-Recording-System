How Attendify works

Four steps from registration to audit-ready attendance, without typing every name by hand.

1

Lecturer sets up

Create a programme, add courses, and share a registration link with students.

2

Students enroll

They confirm index, photo, and download a permanent QR attendance card.

3

Live session scan

Start attendance, scan QR codes (or manual suffix), see photos instantly.

4

Reports & export

Week-by-week grids, duplicate protection, and CSV for faculty records.

Every activity in the system

Core flows at a glance. Open the app when you are ready.

1

Student registration

Enroll via lecturer link. Profile photo loads from index; one QR works for all courses in the programme.

2

QR attendance card

Encrypted token in the QR. No course data exposed. Optional photo in the center of the card.

3

Start live session

Pick week and lecture; HUD scans with sounds for present, duplicate, or error.

4

Weekly reports

Semester grid and CSV export for department audits.
